# Hand sign reference images

The six `*.svg` files in this folder are line drawings of real hands (each
"vectorized from a photograph") by **Schlum**, published on Wikimedia Commons
under the **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0** licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). They are included
unmodified and only renamed:

| tiger.svg | Mudra-Naruto-Tigre.svg | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mudra-Naruto-Tigre.svg |

If you edit these images, your edited versions must keep the same licence and credit.
The game shows the credit under each image (from `credits.json`).
